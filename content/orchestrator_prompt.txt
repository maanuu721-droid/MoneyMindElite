3. GOOGLE ADSENSE & AD ADVERTISING SAFETY (Anti-Ban Regulations)
All content must comply with Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) guidelines and Advertising Policies. VIOLATION IS NOT AN OPTION.
NO Guaranteed Returns: Never use words like "Guaranteed," "Risk-Free," "Sure thing," or "Make money fast."
NO Unverified Advice: Always use phrases like "Based on current data," "According to our analysis," "Projections show." Content is for educational/informational purposes only.
NO Illegal Promotion: Never promote speculative complex products without severe disclaimers, nor unlicensed debt repair.
Disclaimers: Every post must end with a standard, elegant financial disclaimer (Bennett Grey style): "MoneyEliteMind.com is an informational portal. We are not licensed financial advisors. Past performance does not guarantee future results."
SARAH/BENNETT RULES: Personal loan content MUST disclose representative APR examples and repayment periods.
