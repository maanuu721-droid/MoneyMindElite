Asymmetric Risk in Crypto: Navigating the Market Structure

Most crypto participants are gambling. The sophisticated investor treats the blockchain as a giant, transparent ledger of sentiment and liquidity. Market structure is the only truth.

The Liquidity Vacuum

When a coin goes viral, the elite have already taken profits. We look for accumulation phases in the underlying protocols that power the ecosystem, not just the tokens themselves. Asymmetric risk means finding setups where the downside is capped by fundamental utility and the upside is uncapped by market hysteria.
