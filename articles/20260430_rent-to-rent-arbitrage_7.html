Rent-to-Rent Arbitrage: Control $50K Without Owning

Published on April 30, 2026 · MoneyMindElite Research Team

What Is Rent-to-Rent?

Lease a property below-market, sublease it higher on Airbnb/VRBO. The difference is profit. Legal. Scalable.

'I control 12 properties, net $14,000/month. I own zero.' — R2R investor, Phoenix

Real Numbers

  • Rent paid: $2,200/month
  • Airbnb revenue (70% occupancy): $4,800/month
  • Operating costs: $800/month
  • Net profit: $1,800/month per property

Finding Landlords

  • Target listings vacant 30+ days
  • Offer guaranteed rent
  • Show LLC and management agreement

Best Markets 2025

Phoenix, Nashville, Jacksonville, Scottsdale - low regulation, high demand.
